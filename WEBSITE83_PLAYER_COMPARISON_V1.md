# NBA PER-75 Website83 — Player Comparison V1

Built on stable Website82 player profiles.

## Included
- Functional Compare route.
- Search/select two canonical players.
- Regular Season / Playoffs.
- Inclusive start/end season range.
- Season / Era / Historical percentile context.
- 46-stat comparison table.
- Difference column.
- Per-75 statistics use possession weighting when available, otherwise minute weighting.
- Additive statistics are summed.
- Shooting/ratio statistics use natural denominators.
- Remaining rate/impact statistics are minute-weighted.
- Season percentiles are weighted across the selected seasons.
- Backend comparison route: `/api/v1/compare`.

- Final visual redesign.

Those can be added after this functional comparison contract is validated.
