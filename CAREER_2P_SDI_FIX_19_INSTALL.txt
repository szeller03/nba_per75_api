WEBSITE240 — CAREER / 2P% / CREATION SDI FIX 19

This is a replacement for the previous Fix 18 patch. Apply Fix 19 only.

FIXES
1. Career statistics use the audited availability-aware career table.
   - Restores historical 2PA/75 and other recorded pre-1979 per-75 values.
   - Restores career 2P% and its career percentile.
   - Missing tracking years are not treated as zero.

2. Historical season 2P% percentiles.
   - Adds the audited pre-1979 season-relative 2P% percentile layer.
   - Season values remain compared against that specific season's population, not an all-season historical population.

3. Historical season 2PA/75.
   - When the precomputed per-75 field is absent but raw attempts and estimated possessions exist, the API calculates the recorded 2PA/75 value.

4. Career Creation / Playmaking.
   - The locked top-level Creation / Playmaking weight remains 18% of SDI.
   - Creation Output remains 38.5% of the category: AST/75 80% + AST% 20%.
   - Ball Security remains 31.5% of the category, but AST:TOV is now 100% of that group.
   - TOV% is removed from this category. It was double-counting turnover cost already represented by AST:TOV and was disproportionately suppressing elite high-assist creators.
   - WOWY Offense remains 30% of Creation / Playmaking.
   - All other locked top-level SDI weights remain unchanged.

5. Career row / SDI rendering.
   - Career table uses the primary Career profile response directly in Career context.
   - Career six-dimension values use the authoritative career SDI category axes.
