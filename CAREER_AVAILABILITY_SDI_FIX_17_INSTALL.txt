WHAT THIS FIXES
1. Career statistics no longer treat untracked historical seasons as zero.
   The canonical one-row-per-player-season profile layer is used to determine
   whether a statistic was actually recorded.
2. Per-75 counting statistics are recomputed from recorded raw totals and
   recorded estimated possessions only.
3. FG%, 2P%, 3P%, FT%, TS%, rTS, AST:TOV, FTr and 3PAr use their natural
   recorded denominators.
4. OREB%, DREB%, AST%, STL%, BLK%, TOV%, PER, BPM, OBPM and DBPM use only
   observed seasons and are minutes-weighted.
5. OWS, DWS and VORP are summed from observed career seasons.
6. Wilt's career BLK/75 therefore uses only his recorded 1972-73 blocks:
   approximately 4.05 BLK/75, rather than 0.30.
7. Career 2P% is reconstructed from 2P/2PA instead of remaining blank.
8. Career SDI endpoint now prefers the complete WOWY-aware v2 cache.
9. Career Player Profile six-dimension axes use the authoritative WOWY-aware
   career SDI category values, so the UI does not reconstruct them from an
   incomplete career percentile layer.
