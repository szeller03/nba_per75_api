# V31 — Playoff Career Statistical Layer

Creates one career row per canonical Player_ID from the complete 1952-2026
playoff statistical layer.

Career rates are weighted correctly from underlying career totals and career
estimated possessions.

This layer does not calculate percentile qualification. That remains a
separate step so career statistics can exist even when a player does not meet
the percentile threshold.

No identity changes.
No fuzzy matching.
No silent guesses.
