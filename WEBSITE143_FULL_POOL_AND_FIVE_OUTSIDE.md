# Website143 — Full Candidate Pool + Five Just Outside Cutoff

## Final display
Each mode now reveals:
- The requested list itself (T10/T25/T50/T75)
- Exactly five additional players immediately outside that cutoff

Examples:
- T10 → #1–10 plus #11–15
- T25 → #1–25 plus #26–30
- T50 → #1–50 plus #51–55
- T75 → #1–75 plus #76–80

No players beyond those five are shown on the final result screen.

## Full candidate pools
The complete user-defined candidate pool remains active for each mode:
- T10: 20 candidates
- T25: 45 candidates
- T50: 84 candidates
- T75: 114 candidates

The matchup selector now has a first-pass coverage rule: every candidate in
the selected pool is guaranteed to appear in at least one matchup before the
engine begins its normal adaptive/borderline-focused selection. This ensures
the additional candidates are actually utilized rather than some candidates
never entering the decision process.

The duplicate-matchup protection from Website142 remains in place.
