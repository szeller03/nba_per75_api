# Website157 — AST:TOV qualification repair

- Single-season AST:TOV now requires the same BRef basic-stat season
  qualification framework used by the other basic-stat Big Board entries.
- Turnovers must actually be recorded and positive; pre-1977-78 seasons are
  excluded because TOV was not consistently recorded.
- AST:TOV remains AST / TOV and is not converted to a per-game or per-75 stat.
- Career AST:TOV uses only 1977-78 and later seasons.
- Career AST:TOV requires the site's existing career qualification gate:
  at least 400 games and 10,000 minutes in the eligible post-1977-78 sample.
- Career AST and TOV are summed first, then the ratio is calculated; season
  ratios are never averaged.
